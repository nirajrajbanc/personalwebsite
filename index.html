A little about me

I've always been the one taking things apart to understand how they work.

That habit never really left — it just grew up into a full-blown fascination with everything IT. Somewhere between fixing a stubborn Wi-Fi router and staying up too late reading about how the internet actually works, I realized this wasn't a phase. It's just who I am.

I'm based in Nepal, and most of my free time goes into learning by doing — setting things up, breaking them on purpose, and figuring out why they broke. There's no big grand plan here, just a genuine love for understanding systems, one curious rabbit hole at a time.

If you're the kind of person who also enjoys geeking out over how things work behind the screen, we'll probably get along.
